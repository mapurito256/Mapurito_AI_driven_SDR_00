# Local snapshot - SDR receiver datasheet (illustrative)

> Illustrative local snapshot for the seed case. It stands in for the hardware
> datasheet that the real corpus would hold. The point of the seed case is the
> literal-citation mechanism, not the accuracy of these figures.

The receiver tunes from 24 MHz to 1766 MHz and connects over USB.
The maximum sample rate is 2.4 MS/s in stable operation.
The device is a general-purpose software-defined-radio front end.
